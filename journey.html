Success Requires Work

For some, professional success might come easy. For others, success comes after a series of trials that will test their mental and physical fortitude.

A strong faith will keep you on a path to success.

Your Path May Not Be Traveled As Planned

After I attended ITT Technical Institute, I was excited to start on a path into a career of technology. I could not find a good fit in the industry right away.

Discouraged, I decided to give retail leadership a chance, because I was skilled in operating a retail store, so I expanded my role there.

After some time without any opportunities to advance my career, I became frustrated and finally began my career to seek knowledge as a technician.

Fate will send you in the right direction.

Fate May Drive You To Find Your Way

I began my career as a technician, and enjoyed the challenge it gave me and it was able to to give me a chance to serve people at a more significant level

In my career in telecommunications, I had a great sense of accomplishment in helping to provide television, telephone service and internet service to many during the COVID 19 pandemic.

My telecommunications career seemed to be my avenue to finally having rapid and steady advancement. Unfortunately, an injury put my career in danger and caused uncertainty.

Challenges will define our strength.

Do Not Let Uncertainty Discorage You

After the rehabilitation of my knee was complete, I wondered what I could possibly do successfully. I took roles to test the strength of my injured knee and wondered what my next step would be.

One day while pondering my future, I a found a job listing that was mysterious, but seemed like a great opportunity for me.

The new role that I chose would not appear to be for me, but it utilized all of the skills that I have learned in my former roles as a technician, and I was able to excel.

My new role has challenged me to support a team across the country and has given me an opportunity to study at Point University.

Success can come to you mysterious ways. Embrace the opportunity and use it to make you a stronger individual.
