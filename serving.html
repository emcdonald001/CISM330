From a young age, I have been someone that has wanted to do things that would result in the joy or satisfaction of others.

At age 13, my first job was as a baker for a local bakery. I had difficulty picking up routines and procedures in baking, my confidence rose once I received compliments from our customers.

Another job that I have had was a bicycle technician. This job was very valuable to me, because I was able to experience the joy in our customers after receiving a product

As a technician, I have been able to do many things, from helping a customer upgrade their home theater setup in their living room, or help a business display vital information on their screens, and helping people be able to interact with others during a global pandemic.

Although the list that I have provided seems extensive, there is quite a bit more I have done for others, and I am no where near stopping in my quest to help others succeed.
